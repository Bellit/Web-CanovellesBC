## Scope

Home page (`src/pages/index.astro`) of Canovelles BC v2. Mode: Persuade. Code-led build (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action

New families in Canovelles on a phone. Job: see that there is a group for their child's age, where it trains, and sign up. Primary action: the pre-registration form, in working form on the page (submits by composing an email until a form service exists). Avoid: childish, template look, too much text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a blank FCBQ match scoresheet (acta) with one empty roster line waiting for the visitor's child. It refuses the sports-club default: dark full-bleed hero, condensed uppercase headline, row of equal team cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool printed-form paper (#F3F5F2) ruled in one petrol-green form ink (#0F4C4A) with hairline cell grids and solid petrol header bars; scorer's ballpoint blue (#2141B8) and red (#D23B2A) as the only "hand" marks (circles, ticks, slashes, handwritten notes in a biro script face); crest orange (#F28C3A) only inside the crest and ball. Printed type: Archivo grotesque with tabular figures, labels as small form captions. The crest always sits on paper, as the club's seal.

STORY: The parent recognises the acta from the pavilion, reads "Falta un nom a l'acta", types the child's birth year, sees the scorer circle the matching group, and signs the pre-registration box at the foot of the sheet.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Petrol header bar with form title and season. Below, the acta header grid: crest seal top-left in its own cell, fields Lloc / Temporada / Equips / Des de. Left 7 columns: H1 at ~clamp(44px, 7vw, 104px) in Archivo, one-line sub, the birth-year field and "Preinscriu-te" button. Right 5 columns: real club photo stapled to the sheet, slightly rotated, with a biro caption. On mobile the photo drops below the action.

FORM: Candidate 6 of my grounded list (FCBQ acta del partit), assigned by the roll. Seed key 805c253d. Raises: empty roster row as invitation (drum-machine step row), real photos as the event (16mm film), imperfect scorer marks left standing (expedition ice press). Signature interaction: birth-year field → scorer's blue circle draws around the matching roster row and the form's group field fills in; motion grammar: pen strokes drawing on (stroke-dashoffset), the running-score column slashed as the page scrolls.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
