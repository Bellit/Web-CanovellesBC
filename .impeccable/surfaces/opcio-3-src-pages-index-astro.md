# Opció 3 · portada + preinscripció

Scope: home page and pre-registration page of proposal 3. Mode: Persuade. Audience: parents on a phone, daylight, deciding whether their child fits and how to join. User asked for a conventional website structure, creative colour combination, soft entrance motion on sections, cards and buttons, and ONE section that is different.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the floor of the Pavelló Municipal: flat sports-floor colour fields with painted court lines that frame every section. It refuses the navy-and-gold photo hero with a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Synthetic pavilion floor colours used as whole fields: court blue #1f4fbf, zone orange #ef5a2e, outer green #1f8a5b, line yellow #ffd23f, line white, ink #0f1b3d, pale floor grey #eef0f3 as the quiet ground. Lines are 3-4px painted strokes with round ends; arcs, keys and the centre circle are the only ornaments. Anybody (variable width) for display, Hanken Grotesk for text. Buttons are painted rectangles with a line inset.

STORY: A parent sees a real club photo on court, understands "basketball from 4 years old, in Canovelles", types the birth year, sees the group placed on the court, and taps Preinscripció.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left 55%: headline in Anybody wide, short line, primary button "Preinscripció" and secondary "Troba el seu grup". Right: club photo set inside a painted key (zone rectangle + free-throw arc) on court blue, the arc drawing itself on load. Nav: crest, 4 links, painted button.

FORM: Pavilion multisport floor lines; my rank 1 (Impeccable's pick, chosen by user); seed key a12b42eb.

SIGNATURE: "Troba el seu grup" is the different section: a top-down half court in SVG where the ten groups sit as positions from the rim (Escoleta) out to half court (Veteranes); typing a birth year rolls a ball to that group's spot and its details open beside it. Motion grammar: lines paint themselves (stroke-dashoffset) as sections enter, once; cards rise 12px with exponential ease-out; nothing else moves.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
