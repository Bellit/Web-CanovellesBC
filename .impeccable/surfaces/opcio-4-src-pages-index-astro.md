# Opció 4 · portada + preinscripció

Scope: home page and pre-registration page of proposal 4. Mode: Persuade. Audience: parents on a phone; the user asked for maximum originality, total freedom.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The whole site is the scoreboard of the Pavelló Municipal de Canovelles: a lacquered steel cabinet of bulb dot-matrix displays, LOCAL / VISITANT plates and foul panels. Scroll is the game clock: each section is a period. It refuses the photo hero, the card grid and any neon-on-black glow.

OWN-WORLD: Cabinet graphite #14161a with steel bezel #2a2d33; lit bulbs amber #ffb21f and red #ff4a2e, unlit bulbs visible as dim dots behind every digit; white vinyl-cut lettering #f2efe6 for plates; one blue vinyl stripe #2b5cff for the home side. Doto (dot matrix) for every display, Sofia Sans Extra Condensed for vinyl plates, Sofia Sans for running text. No gradients, no glow halos; bulbs are dots.

STORY: A parent lands on the board: LOCAL CANOVELLES BC, 1995 in the big digits, 18 teams in the foul box. Scrolling plays the periods (club, escola, grups, on som). In the grups period they key in the birth year on the console and the board lights the group. The TEMPS MORT button takes them to pre-registration.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width cabinet: top plate LOCAL with crest | big clock digits counting up to 1995 | VISITANT plate reading "LA TEVA FAMÍLIA". Below: period indicator, 18 in a two-digit panel labelled EQUIPS, and the red TEMPS MORT · PREINSCRIPCIÓ push button. Nav as vinyl labels on the cabinet top rail.

FORM: Pavelló scoreboard; my rank 3 (the roll); seed key 0d9c4546. Raises: one-direction time from labanotation (scroll advances the clock); single dot face at two sizes from cracktro.

SIGNATURE: The club photo rendered in canvas as the scoreboard's bulb matrix (amber dots sized by luminance) that resolves into the real photo on hover or tap. Motion grammar: digits tick like a clock with no easing; bulbs switch on in rows; no sliding.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
